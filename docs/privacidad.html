Spoony Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026.

Summary, in one sentence: without creating an account, your data never leaves your phone. If you create an account (optional), the minimum needed for it to work is also stored in the cloud, never sold or used for advertising, and you can delete it whenever you want.

Who is responsible for your data

Jaime Lillo, Spoony's developer, decides what the data this app collects is used for. You can write to krux.app.info@gmail.com with any questions or to exercise the rights explained below.

Without an account, nothing is collected

Spoony works without signing up. Everything you log — tasks, spoons, notes, the Cycle module or the Finances module if you turn them on — stays stored only on your phone. It never goes through any server; neither we nor anyone else sees it.

With an account (optional), what is collected

Creating an account is voluntary and lets you also have a cloud copy you can recover on another phone. If you create one, this is stored:

  • Your email address, to identify the account.
  • The content you log in the app: tasks, notes, and expenses if you have the Finances module turned on.
  • Health data, only if you turn on the Cycle module: bleeding days and pain scores. Turning that module on is itself your explicit consent to process this kind of data; you can turn it off whenever you want, and that stops the collection (what's already stored keeps existing until you delete it yourself).

If you sign in with Google or Apple instead of email and password, we receive from them the email associated with that account — the process of verifying your identity is handled directly by Google or Apple, Spoony never sees your password for those services.

Where it's stored

The cloud copy (only for those who create an account) is hosted on Supabase, on servers within the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Supabase acts as a data processor: it only processes the data following our instructions and never uses it for its own purposes.

What it's used for

Only to make the app work: showing you your own data and keeping it in sync across your devices if you use more than one. Spoony:

  • Doesn't use your data for advertising.
  • Doesn't sell it or share it with third parties for commercial purposes.
  • Doesn't include third-party analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • Doesn't build profiles about you beyond what the app itself shows you (the Patterns section).

How long it's kept

What's on your phone, until you uninstall the app or use "Delete everything" in Settings. What's in the cloud, for as long as your account stays open — it's deleted immediately if you delete your account.

Your rights

  • Access and copy: the "Export" button in Settings gives you a file with your whole history, any time.
  • Correction: you can edit or delete any data directly within the app.
  • Deletion: "Delete my account" in Settings deletes the account and the cloud copy (what's on the phone stays, separately). "Delete everything" deletes the phone's history.
  • Withdrawing consent: turning off the Cycle module at any time stops the collection of that health data.

If any of this doesn't work as described, write to us — that's a failure on our part, not an excuse.

Minors

Spoony isn't specifically aimed at minors and doesn't ask for or verify age or identity documents. If you're a minor, ask an adult responsible for you to decide whether creating an account is appropriate before doing so.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes significantly, we'll let you know inside the app itself before it takes effect.
